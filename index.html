About LifeLink

LifeLink is a smart blood donation management system designed to connect blood donors, blood banks, hospitals, and patients on one secure platform. Our mission is to ensure that blood is available whenever and wherever it is needed.

Through digital technology, LifeLink makes blood donation easier, faster, and more efficient while encouraging more people to become life-saving donors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate blood?

Healthy individuals aged 18–65 years and meeting basic eligibility criteria can donate blood.

How often can I donate?

Most donors can donate whole blood every 3 months, depending on local guidelines.

Is blood donation safe?

Yes. Sterile, single-use equipment is used for every donation, making the process safe.
